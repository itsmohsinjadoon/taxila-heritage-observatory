## Mathematical strengthening already introduced

The revision now states the index sets and score domain, empirical-percentile transform, endpoint adverse-tail convergence statistic, Theil-Sen estimator, Mann--Kendall statistic, false-discovery adjustment, acquisition-window weather aggregation, topographic wetness proxy, simplex-constrained priority model, Dirichlet rank simulation, top-k frequency, rank interval, Spearman agreement, top-k overlap and maximum rank displacement. These equations formalise analyses that were already executed; they do not manufacture new results.

## Non-negotiable evidence boundary

No equation, statistical test or additional prose can substitute for independent archaeological-condition observations. Until such observations exist, CHIP estimates relative inspection priority conditional on the analysed inputs and assumptions, not damage probability, deterioration rate, absolute risk or conservation effectiveness.
