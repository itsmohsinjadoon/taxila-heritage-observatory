# Missing evidence and hard boundaries

## Scientific evidence that is not available

1. Local meteorological station observations for validating Open-Meteo or NASA POWER.
2. Independently verified, complete heritage-condition labels for the mapped components.
3. An M3 condition-linked heritage model.
4. A verified geometry for Saraikala, component 139-002.
5. Official property polygons and legal or UNESCO buffer boundaries for component-level spatial analysis.
6. Field-validated drainage, runoff, erosion or topographic-wetness measurements.
7. A documented expert elicitation for indicator or domain weights.
8. Multiple geographically independent buffered test directions for the proxy-model experiment.
9. A harmonised and validated event inventory for earthquakes, floods, landslides, quarry activity or population pressure.
10. Calibrated future climate projections, future hazard scenarios, structural vulnerability data, exposure inventories or loss functions.

## Consequences for the manuscript

- The paper can assess relative landscape pressure, terrain and hydrological susceptibility, climate forcing and relative field-inspection priority.
- It cannot claim confirmed monument deterioration, a probability of damage, absolute risk, causal climate effects, future damage, economic loss or a legal conservation designation.
- The historical climate trends may inform a monitoring outlook, but must not be extrapolated as a future forecast.
- The four weather cells must remain at their native gridded support. They cannot be represented as 30 m weather.
- Saraikala remains in the documentary inventory but is excluded from coordinate-dependent analysis.
- The 250, 500 and 1000 m circles are analytical sampling supports only.
- The proxy-model experiment measures WorldCover-derived proxy land-cover agreement only.
- The five-epoch weather-spectral analysis remains exploratory and descriptive.
