Figure 1. Study-area and analytical-support context for the Taxila World Heritage property. Panels (a) and (b) locate Taxila within Pakistan and the regional administrative context; panel (c) shows the 17 components for which the UNESCO World Heritage Centre publishes point coordinates. Saraikala (139-002) remains in the official 18-component inventory but is not mapped because the UNESCO geographical-data table does not publish a coordinate. Cyan circles denote the primary 500 m analytical neighbourhoods; 250 m and 1,000 m supports were also analysed. These circles, point locations and the displayed map extent are analytical supports, not official property polygons, statutory protection zones or UNESCO buffer boundaries. Esri World Imagery is used only as a contextual basemap and did not enter the Landsat, terrain, climate or ranking calculations. Imagery sources: Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics and the GIS User Community (accessed 30 August 2026). Road and museum data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence. Metric processing used WGS 84 / UTM zone 43N (EPSG:32643).
