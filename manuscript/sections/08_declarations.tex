\section*{Permissions, site access and ethics}
The analyses used publicly available satellite, elevation and reanalysis products and the published UNESCO inventory. No fieldwork or physical intervention was undertaken, so no site permit was required. Analytical neighbourhoods are not official property or buffer-zone polygons; inventory coordinates retain their published precision.

\section*{Sex and gender in research}
The study analyses environmental observations and archaeological components, with no human participants or individual-level data. Sex and gender are not variables in this study; SAGER reporting is inapplicable.

\section*{Data and code availability}
Processed data and analysis code are available in a public version-controlled repository. Its URL is provided on the editor-facing title page and withheld here for double-anonymised review. The repository contains frozen derived rasters and tables, model and uncertainty outputs, code, configurations, source manifests and checksums. Original third-party Landsat and WorldCover payloads are not redistributed; identifiers and acquisition scripts document their reacquisition. Code is MIT-licensed, original derived data CC BY 4.0, and third-party materials retain provider terms. No repository DOI has been assigned.

During manuscript preparation, the authors used OpenAI ChatGPT only to polish the English wording. The authors reviewed the suggested changes and take full responsibility for the final text. The research design, data processing, code, analyses, figures, results and interpretations were completed by the authors.
