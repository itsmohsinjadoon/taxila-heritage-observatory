# Suggested reviewers — candidates for Editorial Manager

The Journal of Cultural Heritage asks submitting authors to suggest possible
reviewers. Editorial Manager requires, for each: full name, institutional
affiliation, institutional e-mail address, and a one-line reason.

**Every candidate below was selected from this manuscript's own reference list**,
so the relevance is verifiable from the bibliography. Their names and the cited
work are accurate. **Affiliations and e-mail addresses are deliberately left
blank: you must look each one up on the person's current institutional page and
paste the address yourself.** Do not submit a guessed address — Editorial Manager
rejects invalid reviewer e-mails and an incorrect one reflects badly on the
submission.

Choose any three. Candidates 1–3 are the closest match to this paper's scope.
